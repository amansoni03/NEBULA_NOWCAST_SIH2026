1. Executive Summary & Problem Need

Standard Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) models (e.g., WRF, GFS) operate on 6–12 hour run cycles with coarse spatial resolutions of 12–25 km. Consequently, rapidly evolving convective events—such as cloudbursts, severe hailstorms, microbursts, and squall lines—develop, peak, and decay before NWP model initialization completes.

NEBULA-NOWCAST bridges this critical 0–6 hour forecasting gap by fusing Doppler Weather Radar (DWR S/C/X-band 1–3 km grids), INSAT-3D thermal IR satellite channels, and ground IoT barometers into a GPU-accelerated PyTorch ConvLSTM deep learning nowcasting engine.

4. Key Innovations & Uniqueness

  • Ground IoT Mesh Barometer Telemetry: Fuses crowdsourced mobile pressure drop telemetry (-3.2 hPa / 5 min) to validate aloft radar microburst predictions at ground level.
  • PostGIS Geofenced Real-Time WebSockets: Automatically pushes alert notifications to disaster authorities when storm cell reflectivity (dBZ > 55) crosses a 5 km district boundary.
  • Dual Sector Impact Matrix: Provides terminal doppler wind shear risk for pilots alongside crop-specific hail size (MESH cm) warnings for local farmers.
  • Vertical Radar Sounding Profile (VAD / RHI 0–16 km): Displays real-time CAPE, CIN, and freezing level melting layers.

5. Future Scope & Scaling Roadmap

  1. Edge AI Radar Deployment: Quantize ConvLSTM model weights for zero-latency deployment on NVIDIA Jetson / Raspberry Pi 5 edge devices directly at DWR radar stations.
  2. ISRO INSAT-3DS Rapid Scan Integration: Ingest 5-minute rapid scan satellite imagery to detect convective initiation before radar echo formation.
  3. Panchayat Siren & Mobile Push Integration: Direct integration with Apple APNs / FCM and village siren towers for 3-second cloudburst alert delivery.
